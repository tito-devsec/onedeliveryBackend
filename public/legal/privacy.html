Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how OneDelivery ("OneDelivery", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the OneDelivery shopping and delivery app, the OneDelivery Driver app, our website onedelivery.co.tz and related services (together, the "Services"). We are based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, and we process personal data in line with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash) and an optional profile photo.
  • Orders and deliveries: items you order, delivery and pick-up addresses, delivery instructions and order history.
  • Seller information: shop name, description, contact details, shop location and product photos and listings.
  • Driver applications: national ID number, driver's licence number, photos of your ID, licence, vehicle and yourself, vehicle details (type, model, year, colour, plate number), home address, city and mobile money account details for payouts.
  • Messages and reviews: chat messages with our support team or with the people involved in your delivery, and the ratings and reviews you write.

Information collected when you use the Services

  • Location: with your permission, your device's location to fill in your delivery address and to show delivery tracking. Drivers: the Driver app collects precise location, including in the background, while you are online or on a delivery, to offer you nearby requests and to show live tracking to the customer and to our support team. Location is not collected while you are offline.
  • Payments: payment status and transaction references from our payment partner. We do not see or store your mobile money PIN or card details.
  • Device and notifications: a push notification token for your device, the app version, device type and operating system.
  • Usage and logs: IP address and technical logs needed to keep the Services secure and working.

2. Sign in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and a unique Google account identifier with us. We use this information only to create your OneDelivery account or sign you in, and to show your name and photo in the app. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we share it only with the service providers we need to run OneDelivery, as described in section 4.

OneDelivery's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove OneDelivery's access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services).

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To take orders, process payments, arrange pick-up and delivery, and show order and delivery status.
  • To review and verify seller and driver applications and to pay sellers and drivers.
  • To send service notifications by push notification, email or SMS (for example order updates, delivery requests, application decisions and security messages).
  • To provide customer support and respond to your messages.
  • To keep the Services safe, prevent fraud and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide the Services:

  • With the people involved in your order: the driver receives the pick-up and drop-off addresses and your name and phone number; the seller receives the order details; customers see their driver's first name, photo, vehicle details and live location during a delivery.
  • With service providers that process data for us: Snippe (mobile money payments), Cloudinary (image storage), Brevo (email and SMS), Google Firebase and Expo (push notifications), Google Maps (maps and directions) and our hosting providers.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law or by a lawful request from authorities, or to protect the rights, property and safety of our users and OneDelivery.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example transaction records for tax and accounting) and information needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud, which we keep only as long as required.

6. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls and secure hosting. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been compromised.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile in the app, or ask us for a copy of your information.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete your account and personal information. See Delete your account.
  • Location and notifications: you can turn these permissions off in your phone's settings at any time; some features (for example delivery tracking or driving) will not work without them.
  • Objection: you can object to processing or withdraw consent where the law allows, by contacting us.

8. Children

The Services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Sellers and drivers must be at least 18 years old.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above, and we will tell you in the app or by email about important changes.
