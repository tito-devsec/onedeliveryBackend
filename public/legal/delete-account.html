Delete your account

Applies to the OneDelivery app and the OneDelivery Driver app

You can ask us to delete your OneDelivery account and the personal information linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address you use for your account to onerockcompany24@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my OneDelivery account".
  2. Say whether it is a customer/seller account (OneDelivery app) or a driver account (OneDelivery Driver app). If you signed up with Google, use that Google email address.
  3. We will reply to confirm the request and may ask you to verify that the account is yours.

Email a deletion request

Please finish or cancel any open orders or deliveries and withdraw any remaining balance before your account is deleted.

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, password, profile photo and Google sign-in link.
  • Saved addresses, wishlist, cart and push notification tokens.
  • Driver and seller application details and documents (ID, licence, vehicle and shop photos).
  • Chat messages and location history linked to your account.

What we keep, and for how long

We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request. Some records are kept longer only where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and resolve disputes, for example payment and order transaction records for tax and accounting purposes. These are kept securely for the period required by law and then deleted. Reviews you wrote may stay visible without your name.

Questions? Contact us at onerockcompany24@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
